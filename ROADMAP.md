# Roadmap

## What the map does today

| Part | State |
|---|---|
| Languages | English and Bulgarian, complete for every text; search works in both at once |
| Terrain | Real relief at every zoom, sharper around Troy; 3D view; views from the peaks towards Troy |
| Reader mode | A bookmark, 191 scenes for all 24 books, a camera that follows, 40 journeys that play on the map |
| Spoiler guard | Later books, later scene titles, give-away markers and the summary of the book being read stay hidden |
| People | Cards for nineteen heroes, seven family trees, the gods by the side they take |
| Shape of the war | A strip of the fifty-odd days of the poem, and a schematic battle line in 84 battle scenes |
| Then and now | A slider between the coast of about 1200 BC and today; a "Today" layer with modern towns and borders |
| Themes | Realms of the kings, routes, archaeology, the collapse around 1200 BC, trade, sanctuaries, Linear B, Hittite names and more |
| Honesty | Certainty marks, other candidate locations, schematic things labelled as schematic |
| Use | Links that hold the state, a remembered bookmark, offline use after the first visit |
